Support Petali

Petali stays free. If you would like to help its development, you can send a voluntary contribution by bank transfer. If you would rather not, that is completely fine — nothing about Petali changes for you.

How it works

  • Voluntary. Petali works fully without a contribution. Nobody has to pay.
  • Nothing is unlocked. A payment does not unlock features and is not the purchase of a product.
  • Anonymous. No account is needed. The QR code contains only the amount and the recipient, so a payment is not linked to you.
  • Between you and your bank. You pay with your own banking app. Petali cannot see whether a payment was made.
