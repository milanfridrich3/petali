How it works

Three states, one glance at your screen

The plant's state follows your CPU's temperature and load in real time. Move the slider above and watch the mood — and the advice — change.

Healthy

Blooming and active

Your PC is running at a good temperature with low load. Leaves move, colors stay rich.

Tired

Losing its shine

Higher load or temperature. Leaves droop slightly, animations slow down.

Wilting

Needs your help

Critical values. The plant dries out and Petali gives you a concrete tip on what to do.
